# Paper & Pulse — Today, alternative

**Concept.** The day is printed on warm chart paper. The one dark, glowing object is a bedside monitor: its heart pounds at your measured BPM (period 60/BPM) and runs cool to hot with intensity. Light on purpose: daytime legibility, and the pulse glows because nothing else does.

**Palette.** paper #F3EEE4 · sheet #FBF9F4 · ink #171613 · pine #1C6B58 (readiness) · indigo #4046B8 (sleep) · saffron #C4740F (load) · vermilion #DC432D (heart) · plum #83407F (notes) · monitor #0A0E0F. Zones: #4FE0C0 · #E6D24A · #FFA23C · #FF5443 · #FF4BB0.

**Type.** New York serif for sentences and score numerals, SF Pro Rounded for UI, SF Mono caps for labels (renders use system fallbacks).

**Unlike v3/v4.** Light paper, not a dark instrument; tick rulers and liquid discs replace dials and arcs; out-of-range shows a hollow diamond, never red; numbered lab-report sections.

Try `?bpm=148`, `?hr=stale`, `?state=calibrating`.
