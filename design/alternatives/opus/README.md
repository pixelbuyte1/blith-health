# Blith Today, "Daybook" (opus)

**Concept:** Today as a printed daybook. Warm paper, serif numerals, rulers instead of dials, and the live heart drawn on ECG chart paper. Light on purpose: a daylight check-in, and chart paper needs paper.

**Palette:** paper `#F1EDE4`, sheet `#FBF9F4`, ink `#1B1712` / `#554D42` / `#776E62`, readiness verdigris `#1D6B5C`, sleep indigo `#3C3F9A`, load and movement ochre `#A26E0C`, your notes ballpoint `#2440B8`, heart zones `#8B2A2A → #B5462C → #D65A22 → #E23A26 → #E81E5B`, chart grid `#F2DCD3` / `#E7B9AB`.

**Type:** New York for numerals and sentences, SF Mono for labels. The mockup embeds Source Serif 4 and JetBrains Mono (OFL) as stand-ins.

**Versus v3/v4:** light instead of dark. No dials: hatched "your usual" bands sit on rulers. Numbered newspaper sections. The heart is the hero. One hue per domain, with no traffic lights.
